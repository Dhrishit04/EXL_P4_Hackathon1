# Responsible AI input for ModelPassport

ModelPassport prepares evidence for expert review. A human remains responsible for approving model use. The validation service computes every number deterministically and does not call an LLM. Narrative is supplied by a separate validator agent in factory_core. Reports expose unverified narrative numbers and show whether human sign off is pending. Human reviewer independence and the signed audit trail are enforced by factory_core, outside this service.

Fairness uses approval as the favourable outcome. The demographic parity ratio divides the smallest group approval rate by the largest. Equal opportunity difference is the largest gap in approval among good customers, whose target is zero. Equalized odds difference is the larger of that gap and the approval gap among bad customers. Reports include group sizes, overall and class conditional approvals, observed default rates and mean predicted probabilities. Fairlearn results are cross checked against pandas calculations. Groups below fifty observations remain visible but are excluded from comparisons; missing outcome classes produce null rates and LOW findings. These measures identify disparities requiring investigation. They do not establish causation, justify a lending decision, or certify absence of discrimination. Intersectional groups and uncertainty intervals need additional analysis.

Sex and age band are retained for outcome testing and excluded from challenger inputs. Other features can still act as proxies, so exclusion alone cannot establish fairness. Monitoring is explicitly simulated when verified by its batch hash manifest. A simulated result must not be represented as production experience. The synthetic service dataset contains no real customers. The planned Taiwan 2005 credit card and German 1994 public datasets are not UK portfolios; their age, geography and collection context limit transferability. Public data can still contain sensitive attributes and must be handled under appropriate licensing and access controls. The named OOT dataset is a random holdout, not a true out of time sample.

PSI, CSI, AUC, calibration, binomial and fairness thresholds are configurable illustrative defaults, not regulatory limits. HL rejection alone is at most AMBER because the test is sample size sensitive. A GREEN overall rating is not an approval, and unavailable tests can coexist with GREEN; reviewers must inspect findings. Multiple comparisons, small samples, missingness and population change can make point estimates unstable. Input hashes support reproducibility but do not prove data authenticity. Supplied sign off details are displayed and do not authenticate the signer.

A bank would need representative and licensed longitudinal portfolio data, legal and conduct risk review, intersectional tests with uncertainty estimates, proxy analysis, validated model specific thresholds, independent challenge, business impact and stress testing, robust data lineage, access controls, authenticated approvals, immutable audit retention and ongoing outcome monitoring. Trusted local scoring modules are run with a timeout and import restrictions; production must add operating system isolation, network denial and resource limits before executing untrusted code. The wider product should use a private narrative-model deployment within the bank's tenant; public free-tier services may retain prompts. The team's simplified SS1/23 mapping is a demonstration and not a regulatory assessment.
